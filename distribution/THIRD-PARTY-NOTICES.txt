The DOSBox JavaScript wrapper is modified by scripts/prepare-dos.cjs to
expose its existing partial stdout buffer; neither WASM nor MapMaker binaries
are modified. The matching upstream source archives, patch-generation script,
wrapper source and lockfile are included in distribution/source. To rebuild
the emulator, extract its repository and the DOSBox core at native/dosbox,
then follow the emulator repository's pinned build toolchain/scripts. To
rebuild EzMapMaker's wrappers, use npm ci and the package scripts. Supply
the separately licensed original package in the local mapmaker/ folder as
described in README.md; it is not part of the public source checkout.
